Japan lowers its growth forecast as energy costs rise

Makiko Yamazaki

選文理由：用較簡單的英文理解能源價格、薪資、消費與日本經濟成長。

原文 URL ↗

AI Learning Article

Japan’s government lowered its forecast for real economic growth in fiscal 2026 from 1.3% to 0.9%. The main pressure comes from higher oil and energy prices. Because Japan imports much of its fuel, global price increases quickly affect electricity, transport, food production and many daily services.

The government also reduced its expectations for private consumption and business investment. Families may spend less when electricity bills and food prices rise. Companies may delay investment when energy and transport costs reduce profit margins.

Nominal wages may continue to rise, but the key issue is real purchasing power. A higher salary does not improve living standards when prices rise at nearly the same speed.

繁中

Japan steps into the currency market to support the yen

選文理由：理解匯率干預、利率差與生活成本。

原文 URL ↗

AI Learning Article

Japan moved to support the yen after the currency fell to very weak levels. Currency intervention usually means that the authorities sell foreign currency and buy yen. The immediate goal is to slow a rapid decline and reduce disorderly market movements.

A stronger yen can lower the cost of imported oil, food and overseas travel. However, intervention cannot permanently determine an exchange rate. Interest-rate differences, fiscal expectations and investor confidence still influence the currency over time.

For households, a weaker yen raises imported inflation. For companies, the impact depends on whether they import materials or earn revenue overseas.

繁中解讀

外匯干預可在短期支撐日圓並減少輸入型通膨，但不能取代利率與財政政策。

Key terms

  1. Intervention can change positioning before it changes fundamentals.

  3. We should separate the signaling effect from the transaction effect.

AI Learning Article

The European Union plans to support seven large AI computing facilities. Public funding is intended to attract substantially more private capital for chips, data centers, energy systems and high-performance computing.

Gigafactories could give European researchers and companies access to advanced computing resources. Yet building capacity does not automatically create competitive AI products. Success also requires software, data, talent, affordable electricity and paying customers.

The main risks are construction delays, power constraints, fragmented governance and low utilization. The investment case should therefore be assessed through utilization, research output, commercial adoption and long-term operating costs.

Meta’s AI spending creates a difficult compute allocation problem

選文理由：練習財報、資本配置、商業模式與投資人信任。

原文 URL ↗

AI Learning Article

Meta’s enormous AI investment has created an allocation problem: scarce computing resources must be divided among advertising systems, recommendation engines, generative-AI products, research and long-term model development.

Investors may accept high capital expenditure when management can show a credible connection between compute, user engagement, advertising efficiency and future revenue. Infrastructure spending arrives immediately, while commercial returns may appear years later.

Management therefore needs an explicit capital-allocation framework. Near-term projects should be measured through incremental revenue and cost savings; longer-term research should be evaluated through strategic options and technical milestones.

繁中解讀

重點不是要不要投資 AI，而是如何分配稀缺算力，並把資本支出連到未來收入與自由現金流。

AI Learning Article

Researchers examined human hippocampal tissue and found that immune-related activity may change substantially during midlife. Microglia help remove debris and regulate inflammation.

The study identified patterns that may indicate a transition rather than a smooth, linear process. This may help researchers decide when to study preventive interventions.

The evidence remains preliminary. The sample was small, tissue was collected after death, and the design cannot establish that the observed cellular changes cause cognitive decline.
