        <h3>AI Learning Article</h3>
        <div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Meta’s second-quarter free cash flow fell to $784 million, a 91% decline from a year earlier, as the company accelerated spending on chips, servers, energy and data centres. The market reaction was severe: the shares fell more than 9%. The immediate concern was not whether computing capacity has value, but whether Meta has a sufficiently clear path to convert that capacity into durable, high-margin revenue.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>Meta 第二季自由現金流降至 7.84 億美元，年減 91%，主因是晶片、伺服器、能源與資料中心支出加速，股價下跌逾 9%。市場不是懷疑算力有價值，而是質疑 Meta 是否有清楚路徑把算力轉化為持久的高毛利收入。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>Metaの第2四半期フリーキャッシュフローは7億8400万ドルとなり、前年同期比91％減少しました。チップ、サーバー、電力、データセンターへの支出が加速し、株価は9％超下落しました。市場が疑問視したのは計算資源の価値ではなく、高収益で持続的な売上へ転換する道筋です。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Management presented compute as a scarce strategic asset. Meta can use it internally to train models, power personal assistants and build business agents, or it can rent capacity to external customers at a premium. The allocation decision is difficult because both options appear attractive. External sales could relieve the cash-flow squeeze and prove that the assets have market value. Internal use could create more valuable products, but the future revenue model remains less defined.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>管理層將算力視為稀缺戰略資產，可用於內部模型、個人助理與企業代理，也能高價出租。外售能改善現金流並證明資產市場價值；內部使用則可能創造更高價值產品，但收入模式仍不明確。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>経営陣は計算資源を希少な戦略資産と位置づけています。自社モデル、個人向けアシスタント、企業エージェントに使うことも、外部へプレミアム価格で貸すこともできます。外販はキャッシュフローを改善しますが、自社利用はより高い製品価値を生む可能性があります。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>This is where Meta differs from Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon. Those companies can direct data-centre investment toward established cloud platforms with large corporate customer bases. Meta still earns most of its money from advertising. Every additional infrastructure dollar therefore depends on the advertising business until subscriptions, enterprise agents or compute sales become material. One analyst described the company as spending like a hyperscaler without possessing the same business model.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>Meta 與 Microsoft、Alphabet、Amazon 的差異，在於後者有成熟雲端平台與企業客群。Meta 主要仍依賴廣告收入，因此在訂閱、企業代理或算力銷售成為實質收入前，基礎設施支出仍由廣告業務承擔。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>Microsoft、Alphabet、Amazonは既存クラウドと法人顧客を持ちますが、Metaの収益は依然広告中心です。新規サービスが大きな売上になるまで、インフラ投資は広告事業に依存します。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The company argues that the industry historically underbuilt capacity and that shortages will persist. It also raised its capital-spending range to $130–145 billion. Investing before demand is visible can be rational because data centres take years to plan and construct. However, the strategy increases execution risk. Demand could develop more slowly than expected, newer chips could improve efficiency, or competitors could offer cheaper models and services.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>公司認為產業過去建設不足，算力短缺將持續，並把資本支出區間提高至 1,300–1,450 億美元。提前投資有合理性，但也提高執行風險：需求可能較慢、晶片效率可能提升、競爭者可能提供更便宜產品。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>Metaは業界の計算資源が歴史的に不足してきたと主張し、設備投資見通しを1300億～1450億ドルへ引き上げました。先行投資には合理性がある一方、需要の遅れ、チップ効率向上、低価格競争などの実行リスクがあります。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Investors are also influenced by Meta’s previous metaverse spending. The comparison is not exact, because AI already contributes to advertising and recommendation systems. Nevertheless, the metaverse episode makes shareholders more sensitive to broad visions without measurable milestones. To rebuild confidence, Meta needs to define a capital-allocation framework: how much compute is reserved for internal products, what return is expected from external sales, and which operating indicators will demonstrate progress.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>投資人也受到 Meta 過去元宇宙支出的影響。AI 已實際改善廣告與推薦，兩者不能完全類比，但元宇宙經驗使市場更要求明確里程碑。Meta 需要說明內外部算力分配、預期報酬與進度 KPI。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>過去のメタバース投資も市場心理に影響しています。AIは広告や推薦ですでに価値を生んでいますが、投資家は測定可能なマイルストーンを求めます。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The central lesson is that a valuable asset is not automatically a good investment at any price. Strategic scarcity must eventually appear in revenue, margins or defensible capabilities. Until that link becomes more concrete, the market is likely to keep applying a higher risk discount to Meta’s AI ambition.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>核心教訓是，有價值的資產不代表任何價格下都是好投資。稀缺性最後必須反映在收入、利潤率或可防禦能力上。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>希少な資産であっても、どの価格でも良い投資になるわけではありません。最終的には売上、利益率、競争優位に結び付く必要があります。</p></div></div>
