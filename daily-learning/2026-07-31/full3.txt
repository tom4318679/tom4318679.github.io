        <h3>AI Learning Article</h3>
        <div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Japan appears to have intervened in the foreign-exchange market by buying yen and selling dollars in New York. A market source said it was the first intervention in about three months. The action came after the yen had fallen to levels not seen in four decades, increasing the cost of imported energy and other goods.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>日本似乎在紐約外匯市場買進日圓、賣出美元，進行約三個月來首次干預。日圓先前跌至四十年低點，使進口能源與商品成本上升。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>日本はニューヨーク市場で円買い・ドル売り介入を行ったとみられます。約3カ月ぶりの介入で、円が40年ぶりの安値圏まで下落し、輸入エネルギーや商品の価格を押し上げていたことが背景です。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Currency intervention can create a sharp short-term move because the government enters the market with very large orders. However, intervention does not automatically change the economic forces that weakened the currency. If Japanese interest rates remain much lower than rates overseas, investors may still prefer dollar-denominated assets. That is why markets quickly shifted their attention to the Bank of Japan.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>匯率干預能因政府的大額交易而造成短期劇烈波動，但不會自動改變導致日圓走弱的經濟力量。若日本利率仍遠低於海外，投資人可能繼續偏好美元資產，因此市場很快把焦點轉向日本銀行。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>為替介入は巨額注文により短期的な変動を生みますが、円安をもたらした経済要因を自動的に変えるわけではありません。日本の金利が海外より低い状態が続けば、投資家はドル建て資産を選び続ける可能性があります。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The BOJ was widely expected to keep its policy rate at 1%, while signaling that further rate increases remained possible. Governor Kazuo Ueda faced a difficult balance. Raising rates could support the yen and reduce imported inflation, but higher borrowing costs could also weaken consumption, housing and business investment. The government, meanwhile, has been cautious about rapid rate increases.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>市場普遍預期日銀維持政策利率在 1%，同時暗示未來仍可能升息。升息可支撐日圓並降低輸入型通膨，但也可能抑制消費、房市及企業投資，形成艱難取捨。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>日銀は政策金利を1％に据え置きつつ、追加利上げの可能性を示すとみられていました。利上げは円を支え輸入インフレを抑える一方、消費、住宅、設備投資を弱めるおそれがあります。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Japan had already spent a record amount on intervention between late April and early May, but the effect did not last. This history shows that intervention can slow disorderly market moves, yet it may not reverse a long-term trend without supportive monetary and fiscal policy. For households, a weaker yen is not only a financial-market issue. It affects fuel, food, travel and the price of imported products.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>日本在四月底到五月初已投入創紀錄資金干預，但效果未能持續。這表示干預能抑制失序波動，卻很難在缺乏貨幣與財政政策配合時扭轉長期趨勢。弱勢日圓會影響燃料、食品、旅行及進口商品。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>日本は4月末から5月初めにも過去最大規模の介入を行いましたが、効果は長続きしませんでした。介入は無秩序な動きを抑制できても、金融・財政政策の裏付けがなければ長期トレンドを反転させにくいことを示します。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The next question is whether policy signals become consistent. If the government tries to support the yen while also discouraging the BOJ from raising rates, markets may see a contradiction. Sustainable currency stability usually requires more than one dramatic day of official buying.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>接下來要看政策訊號是否一致。若政府一方面支撐日圓，另一方面又不希望日銀升息，市場可能認為政策互相矛盾。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>今後は政策メッセージの整合性が焦点です。円を支える一方で日銀の利上げを牽制すれば、市場は矛盾と受け止める可能性があります。</p></div></div>
