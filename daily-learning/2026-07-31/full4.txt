        <h3>AI Learning Article</h3>
        <div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The European Union plans to provide €10 billion for seven large AI computing facilities and hopes to attract at least €20 billion in private investment. The number of planned “gigafactories” was increased from five after strong interest from member states. These projects will combine advanced processors, software, cloud platforms, high-speed networks and data-centre infrastructure.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>歐盟計畫投入 100 億歐元建設七座大型 AI 算力設施，並希望吸引至少 200 億歐元民間投資。因會員國興趣強烈，規劃數量由五座增至七座。設施將整合先進處理器、軟體、雲端、高速網路與資料中心。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>EUは7つの大規模AI計算施設に100億ユーロを投じ、少なくとも200億ユーロの民間投資を呼び込む方針です。加盟国の関心が強かったため、計画数は5から7へ増えました。施設は先端プロセッサー、ソフトウェア、クラウド、高速通信、データセンターを統合します。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The policy reflects a change in how governments view computing capacity. In the past, servers and data centres were mainly treated as private business assets. Today, advanced compute is increasingly described as strategic infrastructure. The EU wants European companies and researchers to have enough capacity to develop large models without relying entirely on American or Chinese ecosystems.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>這反映政府看待算力方式的改變。過去伺服器與資料中心主要被視為私人資產，如今先進算力逐漸被定位為戰略基礎設施。歐盟希望降低對美國與中國生態系的完全依賴。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>この政策は、計算資源に対する政府の見方が変わったことを示しています。従来は民間企業の資産とみられていたサーバーやデータセンターが、現在では戦略インフラとして位置づけられています。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>The programme also illustrates the use of blended finance. Public funding is expected to reduce early risk and encourage technology companies, cloud providers, public bodies and institutional investors to form consortia or special-purpose vehicles. AMD, Nvidia and Qualcomm have signed letters of intent to supply chips, although a letter of intent is not the same as a final purchase contract.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>計畫也運用混合融資。公共資金降低早期風險，促使科技公司、雲端業者、公部門與機構投資人組成聯盟或特殊目的公司。晶片商的意向書不等於最終採購契約。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>公共資金で初期リスクを下げ、テック企業、クラウド事業者、公的機関、機関投資家がコンソーシアムや特別目的会社を作る混合金融の設計です。ただし、半導体各社の意向書は最終契約ではありません。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>Building capacity is only the first stage. The projects will need reliable electricity, cooling, skilled engineers and enough customers to maintain high utilisation. A large facility can become a financial burden if it is completed late, uses outdated chips or operates below capacity. The EU must therefore measure success not only by the number of GPUs installed, but also by research output, commercial adoption and productivity gains.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>建成容量只是第一步，還需要電力、冷卻、工程人才與足夠客戶。若延誤、晶片過時或利用率偏低，設施可能成為財務負擔。因此成果不應只看 GPU 數量，也要看研究、商業採用與生產力。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>建設後には電力、冷却、人材、顧客、稼働率が必要です。完成の遅延、チップの陳腐化、低稼働率は財務負担につながります。GPU数だけでなく研究成果、商用化、生産性で評価する必要があります。</p></div></div><div class='tri-para'><div class='lang en'><h4>EN</h4><p>There is also a strategic tension. Supporting domestic capacity can improve technological sovereignty, but too much protection may weaken competition or lead governments to support inefficient projects. The strongest case for the programme is not that Europe should copy every American investment, but that it should build selected capabilities where public coordination solves a genuine market failure.</p></div><div class='lang zh'><h4>繁中</h4><p>戰略上仍有張力：科技主權可降低依賴，但過度保護可能削弱競爭或補貼低效率項目。最合理的政策邏輯是，公共協調應解決真正的市場失靈。</p></div><div class='lang ja'><h4>日本語</h4><p>技術主権を高める一方、過度な保護は競争を弱める可能性があります。政策の妥当性は、公共協調が真の市場失敗を解決できるかで判断すべきです。</p></div></div>
